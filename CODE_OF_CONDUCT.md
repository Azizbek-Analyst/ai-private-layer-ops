## Code of Conduct

AI Private Layer is committed to building a professional, security-focused, and inclusive open-source community.

This project provides infrastructure for handling PII and encryption, so we maintain high standards of discourse and responsibility.

---

### 🌍 Our commitment

We pledge to make participation:

- Respectful and harassment-free
- Focused on technical merit
- Safe for people working with sensitive and regulated data

---

### 🤝 Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Focus on ideas, not individuals.
- Provide constructive technical feedback.
- Act in good faith and assume others do the same.
- Respect security and responsible disclosure policies.

---

### 🚫 Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling, flame wars, or bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing private information (doxxing).
- Public disclosure of vulnerabilities or exploits without following `SECURITY.md`.
- Sharing real PII of any individual in code, issues, or discussions.

---

### 🔐 Security sensitivity

Because this project handles encryption and PII:

- **Do not** disclose vulnerabilities publicly.
- **Do not** share real production data, secrets, or keys.
- Follow responsible disclosure guidelines in `SECURITY.md`.

---

### ⚖ Enforcement

Project maintainers may:

- Remove or edit comments.
- Close or lock discussions.
- Request changes to PRs or reject them.
- Restrict or ban contributors who violate this Code of Conduct.

We reserve the right to protect the integrity and safety of this security-focused project.

---

### 📧 Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or need to report a concern, contact:

- **Email**: `support@private-layer.ai`

For security vulnerabilities, please follow the process in `SECURITY.md` instead of filing a public issue.

All reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed in good faith.
